Advanced Lyrics View

Sing along to your favorite tracks with comprehensive lyrics support built directly into the player.

Headphone Controls

Never take your phone out of your pocket. Fully supports standard headphone and earbud clicks.

  • 1 Click: Play / Pause
  • 2 Clicks: Next Track
  • 3 Clicks: Previous Track
  • Dedicated volume buttons supported

Take control of your sound. Features a built-in equalizer for a superior and customizable listening experience.
